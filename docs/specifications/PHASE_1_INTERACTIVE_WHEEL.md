# Phase 1 Interactive Wheel Specification
The wheel has 12 angular wedges and two rings: outer B/major and inner A/minor. Number 12 is centered at the top, followed clockwise by 01 through 11. Selection does not toggle off. Compatibility includes same-letter ±1 and opposite-letter same, -3, +3. Energy boost includes same-letter +2, +5, +7. Center-hole and outside touches are ignored.
